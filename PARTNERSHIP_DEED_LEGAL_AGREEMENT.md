### PREAMBLE & RECITAL

**WHEREAS**, the Partners are individuals of sound mind possessing entrepreneurial aptitude in technology, operations, and marketing;  
**AND WHEREAS**, the Partners have mutually agreed to join together in a partnership business to establish, operate, and scale a tech-enabled student accommodation marketplace and verification service under the business and brand name of **“THIKANA”**;  
**AND WHEREAS**, the Partners desire to reduce the terms, conditions, mutual rights, duties, and covenants of their partnership into writing under the provisions of the **Partnership Act, 1932**.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THIS DEED WITNESSETH AND IT IS MUTUALLY AGREED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTNERS AS FOLLOWS:**

---

### ARTICLE 1: NAME OF THE FIRM & BRAND IDENTITY
1.1 The business of the partnership shall be conducted under the official trade name and style of:  
**“M/S THIKANA TECHNOLOGIES”** (hereinafter referred to as the "Firm").  
1.2 The commercial consumer-facing brand and digital platform shall operate under the trade mark and name **“THIKANA”** (with tagline: *"Apna Thikana, Ghar Jaisa"*).  
1.3 Any alteration or modification in the name of the Firm shall only be made with the unanimous written consent of all three Partners.

---

### ARTICLE 2: NATURE AND SCOPE OF BUSINESS
2.1 The primary business of the Firm shall be:
- The design, operation, and maintenance of an online multi-sided digital platform (web application and mobile app) connecting university students and young professionals with physically inspected, verified student hostels and shared apartments.
- Provision of independent on-ground physical inspection, hygiene auditing, security assessment, and award of the proprietary **“Thikana Verified”** certification badge to student accommodations.
- Automated algorithmic roommate compatibility profiling, matching, and university ID credential verification.
- Value-added auxiliary services including tiffin/mess coordination, laundry logistics facilitation, tenant management software, and parent safety monitoring tools.
- Any other allied, ancillary, or commercial business activities as may be mutually agreed upon in writing by all Partners from time to time.

---

### ARTICLE 3: PRINCIPAL PLACE OF BUSINESS
3.1 The principal office and headquarters of the Firm shall be situated at:  
**Suite # 304, 3rd Floor, Al-Hafeez Heights / Khayaban-e-Firdousi, Johar Town, Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan.**  
3.2 The Firm may establish regional branch offices, inspection hubs, or university liaison desks across Lahore, Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Multan, or any other city in Pakistan as business growth demands, subject to majority approval of the Partners.

---

### ARTICLE 4: COMMENCEMENT AND DURATION
4.1 The partnership shall be deemed to have commenced on **June 1, 2025**.  
4.2 The duration of the partnership shall be **“AT WILL”**, subject to the specific exit, retirement, and dissolution covenants set forth in Articles 12, 13, and 14 of this Deed.

---

---

### ARTICLE 6: PROFIT AND LOSS SHARING RATIO
6.1 The net profits of the Firm, after deducting all operational overheads, staff salaries, technology subscriptions, marketing costs, utility bills, taxes, depreciation, and allocations to emergency reserves, shall be divided among the Partners in the following ratio:
- **Saif Ur Rehman Hashmi (CEO):** **33.34%**
- **Nauman Naseer (CTO):** **33.33%**
- **Rohan Khalid Awan (CMO):** **33.33%**

6.2 In the event of any net operational loss, such loss shall be borne by the Partners in the exact same proportion as their profit-sharing ratio, unless caused by the gross negligence, willful misconduct, or unauthorized unilateral acts of a single Partner.  
6.3 The Partners agree that during the initial twelve (12) months of operations, at least **50% of monthly net distributable profits** shall be retained and credited to the Firm’s Business Reinvestment and Capital Reserve Fund to finance marketing and technology scaling.

---

### ARTICLE 7: EXECUTIVE ROLES, MANAGEMENT & RESPONSIBILITIES
To ensure clear accountability, the specific operational domains of each Partner are established as follows:

7.1 **Saif Ur Rehman Hashmi – Chief Executive Officer (CEO) & Head of Operations:**
- Oversees overall company vision, day-to-day administrative operations, and legal/regulatory compliance.
- Directs the on-ground Physical Inspection Team and enforces the 50-point **Thikana Verified** Quality & Safety Audit checklist across Lahore hostels.
- Manages hostel onboarding, field agent recruitment, dispute resolution between students and hostel wardens, and strategic landlord relationships.
- Coordinates external legal counsel, accounting, taxation with FBR and PRA, and government registrations.

7.2 **Nauman Naseer – Chief Technology Officer (CTO) & Head of Product Development:**
- Owns complete end-to-end architecture, development, deployment, and security of the Thikana web platform, mobile PWA/app, and backend databases.
- Designs and optimizes the proprietary algorithmic **AI Roommate Compatibility Matching System** and Student Identity Verification flow.
- Administers cloud infrastructure (AWS/DigitalOcean), data protection protocols, SSL encryption, and API integrations with payment gateways (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, PayFast, NayaPay).
- Maintains platform uptime, mobile responsiveness, cybersecurity, and prevents data breaches of female student records.

7.3 **Rohan Khalid Awan – Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) & Head of Business Development:**
- Spearheads brand positioning, digital advertising campaigns (Meta Ads, Google Search, TikTok), and social media content creation.
- Formulates and leads the **Campus Ambassador Network** across major Lahore universities (UMT, UCP, FAST, LUMS, PU, Kinnaird, COMSATS).
- Sources, negotiates, and finalizes commercial vendor agreements with third-party service providers (hygienic tiffin/mess providers and on-demand laundry vendors).
- Drives customer acquisition, parent awareness seminars, student conversion funnels, and public relations.

### ARTICLE 8: BANKING OPERATIONS & FINANCIAL PROTOCOLS
8.1 A dedicated commercial current bank account shall be opened in the name of **“M/S THIKANA TECHNOLOGIES”** with a reputable scheduled bank in Pakistan (e.g., Meezan Bank Limited, HBL, or Bank Alfalah).  
8.2 **Signing Authority:**
- Routine operational expenditures up to **PKR 30,000/-** may be authorized by the Managing Partner (Saif Ur Rehman Hashmi) or CTO (Nauman Naseer).
- All checks, banking transactions, online funds transfers, credit facilities, or commitments exceeding **PKR 30,000/-** shall strictly require the **JOINT SIGNATURES of at least TWO (2) PARTNERS**.
- Any expenditure or contract exceeding **PKR 150,000/-** shall strictly require the prior written approval and signatures of **ALL THREE (3) PARTNERS**.
8.3 Proper books of accounts, digital ledgers, receipt vouchers, and sales registers shall be maintained at the principal office of the Firm and shall remain open for inspection by any Partner at any reasonable time during business hours.  
8.4 The financial year of the Firm shall commence on the **1st day of July** and terminate on the **30th day of June** of each calendar year. An annual audit of accounts shall be conducted by an independent chartered accountant firm appointed with mutual consent.

---

### ARTICLE 9: DECISION-MAKING & VOTING POWERS
9.1 All ordinary, day-to-day administrative decisions within the defined scope of a Partner’s operational role (per Article 7) may be executed autonomously by that respective Partner.  
9.2 Major policy, commercial, and operational decisions shall be determined by **Majority Vote (at least 2 out of 3 Partners)**, including:
- Hiring or termination of permanent employees and determining salary structures.
- Approving monthly marketing budgets exceeding PKR 50,000.
- Entering into commercial service agreements with hostel chains or vendor partners.
9.3 **Unanimous Consent (All 3 Partners)** shall be mandatory for:
- Alteration of this Partnership Deed or modification of equity/profit-sharing ratios.
- Admission of any new partner, investor, or institutional venture capitalist.
- Borrowing capital, securing bank loans, or mortgaging any company assets.
- Opening or closing company bank accounts.
- Expansion into new geographical cities outside Lahore.
- Sale, merger, intellectual property licensing, or voluntary dissolution of the Firm.

---

### ARTICLE 10: CONFIDENTIALITY & NON-COMPETE COVENANTS
10.1 Each Partner undertakes to maintain strict confidentiality regarding all proprietary source code, algorithmic formulations, verified hostel inspection metrics, database records, customer contact information, landlord agreements, and pricing structures of the Firm.  
10.2 **Non-Compete Covenant:** During the continuation of this partnership and for a period of **twenty-four (24) months** following the retirement, exit, or removal of any Partner, such outgoing Partner shall not directly or indirectly engage in, establish, manage, advise, or invest in any competing student accommodation, hostel booking platform, or roommate matching business within the Province of Punjab.

---

### ARTICLE 11: DISPUTE RESOLUTION & ARBITRATION CLAUSE
11.1 In the event of any disagreement, dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Partnership Deed, the Partners shall first endeavor to resolve the matter amicably through internal mediation and formal consultation within thirty (30) days.  
11.2 If the dispute cannot be resolved through internal mediation, it shall be referred to arbitration in accordance with the provisions of the **Arbitration Act, 1940 (Act X of 1940)** of Pakistan.  
11.3 The arbitration tribunal shall consist of a sole independent arbitrator appointed by mutual agreement of the Partners, or failing such agreement, an arbitrator appointed by the District Court Lahore. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be **Lahore, Pakistan**, and proceedings shall be conducted in the English language. The award of the arbitrator shall be final, binding, and conclusive upon all parties.

---

### ARTICLE 12: RETIREMENT & VOLUNTARY EXIT CLAUSE
12.1 Any Partner intending to retire or exit the Firm shall give at least **ninety (90) days' prior written notice** to the remaining Partners specifying his intention.  
12.2 Upon receipt of an exit notice, the remaining Partners shall have the first right of refusal (Pre-emptive Right) to purchase the outgoing Partner’s equity share in equal proportions.  
12.3 **Valuation and Settlement Formula:**
- The valuation of the outgoing Partner’s interest shall be determined based on the audited Net Asset Value (NAV) of the Firm plus a fair market multiple of trailing twelve months’ EBITDA, assessed by an independent Certified Chartered Accountant (ICAP member).
- The settlement amount due to the exiting Partner shall be paid in **three (3) equal quarterly installments** over a nine (9) month period to prevent liquidity strain on the Firm’s working capital.
- Under no circumstances shall an exiting Partner sell, transfer, or pledge his equity share to an external third party without the express unanimous written consent of the remaining Partners.

---

### ARTICLE 13: ADMISSION OF NEW PARTNERS OR INVESTORS
13.1 No external person, angel investor, or venture capital entity shall be admitted as a new partner into the Firm without the express written and unanimous consent of all existing Partners.  
13.2 Any equity dilution necessitated by external investment shall be borne proportionally by all existing Partners based on their current holding percentages, unless mutually agreed otherwise.

---

### ARTICLE 14: DISSOLUTION OF PARTNERSHIP
14.1 The Firm may be dissolved only by the unanimous written resolution of all three Partners.  
14.2 Upon dissolution, the assets and properties of the Firm shall be liquidated, and the proceeds shall be applied in the following order of priority under Section 48 of the Partnership Act, 1932:
- First, in payment of all third-party debts, liabilities, and outstanding vendor accounts of the Firm.
- Second, in repayment of any advances or loans made by Partners to the Firm beyond their capital.
- Third, in return of the original capital contributed by each Partner.
- Fourth, any surplus balance remaining shall be distributed among the Partners in accordance with their profit-sharing ratio (33.34% / 33.33% / 33.33%).

---

### ARTICLE 15: GENERAL PROVISIONS & STATUTORY REGISTRATION
15.1 This Partnership Deed shall be registered with the **Registrar of Firms, Lahore, Government of the Punjab**, under Section 58 of the Partnership Act, 1932, and the prescribed Form A and Form C shall be submitted accordingly.  
15.2 The Firm shall obtain a National Tax Number (NTN) from the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) and register with the Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA) for provincial sales tax on services.  
15.3 In respect of any matter not specifically addressed in this Deed, the statutory provisions of the **Partnership Act, 1932** and general commercial laws of Pakistan shall apply.
